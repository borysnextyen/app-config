Get help

Found a bug or have an idea? Email borysnextyen@gmail.com. If the app crashed, you can attach the report from Settings > App diagnostics.

Common questions

Why didn't I get a reminder? Check that notifications are on in Settings and in your iPhone's Settings > Notifications > Service Buddy. Mileage reminders use your odometer readings, so update the odometer now and then to keep them on time.

Can I use kilometers? Yes. Go to Settings > Units > Distance.

How do I keep my data when I get a new iPhone? Your data is included in your iPhone backup (iCloud or computer). You can also export your service history and photos from Settings.

I sold my car. What now? Archive it from the car's edit screen. It stops sending reminders and keeps its history, and you can restore it or delete it later.
