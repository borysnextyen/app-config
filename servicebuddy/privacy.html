Service Buddy Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Service Buddy reminds you when your car or home needs maintenance. It's built so your information stays with you. There's no account to create, and we don't run a server that stores your data.

Your data stays on your devices

Your cars, homes, reminders, service history, odometer readings, tire sets and photos are saved on your iPhone. The Apple Watch app gets a copy of your upcoming reminders from your iPhone. We never see this information, and we don't sell or share it.

Reminder notifications are scheduled on your iPhone. They aren't sent from a server.

Photos and camera

If you add a photo of your car or home, the app only receives the photo you pick or take. It shrinks the photo and removes its metadata, such as location, before saving it on your device.

Exports

You can export your service history (CSV) and your photos from Settings. They go only where you choose to share or save them.

Anonymous usage data

To learn which features people use, the app sends anonymous usage events through TelemetryDeck, for example "a service was logged" or "tires were rotated". These events never include anything you type, such as names, notes, VINs or addresses. They include no advertising identifier and aren't used to track you across apps or websites. TelemetryDeck hashes the device identifier on your device, so events can't be traced back to you.

You can turn this off at any time in Settings > Privacy > Share anonymous usage data.

Crash reports

Crash and freeze reports from Apple's MetricKit are saved on your device, under Settings > App diagnostics. They're only sent to us if you choose to share one. If you've agreed to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may also send us anonymous crash reports.

Update checks

The app checks a small public file on GitHub to see whether a newer version is required. The request carries no information about you. Like any website, GitHub may log the request.

Children

Service Buddy isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, for example when iCloud sync is added, we'll update this page and the date above.
